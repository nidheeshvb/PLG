Strategy & Consulting

Sponsorship sales, partnerships, media rights, technology, data and IP commercialisation — the commercial foundation of every programme.

Talk Strategy

  • Sponsorship

    Our core service

    We sell sponsorship on behalf of rights holders, events and venues — valuing and packaging inventory, then taking it to brands in the UAE and internationally. Pricing is set against market benchmarks, not aspiration.

  • Partnerships

    Structuring partnerships between brands and rights holders. For brands, we broker the deal for maximum brand value, then activate around the sponsorship. Commercial terms are built to outlast a single season.

  • Media rights

    Valuation, negotiation and distribution strategy for broadcast and digital media rights, across traditional and emerging platforms.

  • Technology and innovations

    Phygital format development — sport that combines physical competition with gaming mechanics, live data and digital interaction — designed with a commercial model from the start.

  • Data optimisation

    Mujtamae

    Our sports analytics platform, applied at the consulting stage — drawing on sports communities and full-spectrum sports analytics to model audience, engagement and commercial return before a programme is committed.

    How Mujtamae fits

  • Licensing and IP management

    Commercialisation of intellectual property through licensing structures, brand protection and long-term value management.
