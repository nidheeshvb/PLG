The analytics behind the programme.

Why The UAE?

A market in its own right. A base for every region.

The UAE is one of the world’s fastest-growing sports markets, with major international properties, a young and digitally engaged population, and sustained national investment in technology. It is a significant market in its own right, and a strong base from which to work across regions.

Our Ownership

Part of an established group.

PLG Acachet is part of Muller & Phipps Middle East Group Holdings (M&P Group ME), a technology distribution group with sales, marketing and distribution operations across the UAE and wider MEA region. M&P Group ME is part of The Getz Group, an international marketing, distribution and sourcing group founded in 1852 and operating in more than 50 countries.
