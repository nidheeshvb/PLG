Cookie banner (every page)First visit to the siteDecided: ask the visitor to accept or refuse cookies (Cookiebot, free plan)

Please create a free Cookiebot account in PLG's name and add us as a user. Cookiebot also writes the list of cookies for the Cookies page.

The free plan costs nothing. It covers one website with up to 50 pages (ours has about 11).

What the free plan doesn't have (the paid plan, about €7 a month, adds them):

  • the banner is in one language only (English is fine for this site);
  • Cookiebot's standard look: no PLG logo or colours;
  • visitors are asked again every month, instead of once a year;
  • the site isn't re-checked for new cookies every month (we re-check when the site changes);
  • no email support.

Other free options:

  • CookieYes: free up to 5,000 page views a month, then paid (from about $10 a month);
  • Silktide: free with no limits, but keeps no record of visitors' choices, which is useful if you're ever asked to prove consent.

02Who will edit the website?

You'll update the website yourselves in its admin area (/admin). We'll give you one account. With it you can change, add and remove anything on the website, and read the messages sent through the forms.

You can also add other people: open Users, add the person's name and email, set a starting password and give it to them privately (not by email). They can change it after they sign in. Everyone you add has the same full access.

Your answer: the name and email of the person who should get the first account.
