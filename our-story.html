The Combination

PLG Acachet unites three businesses that previously operated separately. Acachet built relationships across UAE government and corporate bodies over more than a decade, delivering city-scale and corporate sport programming. PLG built a decade of esports and phygital delivery — tournaments, broadcast and community programming. Mujtamae built the platform to measure return on sport, esports and phygital activation.
